# Circular 313 — legal map annex availability and 2026 route events (18 November 2026)

## Original 2025 legal annex availability

The original **Gaceta 5667**, Resolución **202550076168/2025**, has been re-inspected at source PDF pages 53–57. Article 3 authorizes two GTPC direction entries **29903 Norte–Sur** and **49904 Sur–Norte**, each length **20.65 km**, and explicitly says the itinerary is defined by a **plan attached to the resolution and by the district GIS**. The official Gaceta PDF does not provide a ready-to-digitize route-map annex, nor any embedded PDF attachment (checked with `pdfdetach -list`: **0 files**). No official geographic coordinates or polyline geometry can be recovered from those pages alone.

This remains consistent with the earlier [P0 projected line audit](2025_p0_shape_and_period_qa_2026-11-18.md): historic ArcGIS route 90004 has **13.180/14.536 km** from the archive, not the official 20.65/20.65 km. Do not create a new shape by inflating or warping the old one to match length, or assume that digitization order `Origen-Destino` equals legal GTPC compass direction.

## Newer official route-specific geometry and stop changes (2026)

1. **26 January 2026: Temporary Metro de la 80 / Caribe detour**. [Metro de Medellín's official announcement](https://www.metrodemedellin.gov.co/al-dia/noticias/desde-este-lunes-26-de-enero-inician-cambios-en-la-movilidad-de-la-zona-de-caribe-para-avanzar-en-trabajos-del-metro-de-la-80) lists 313 specifically among routes diverted due to the closure of Carrera 64B (the 'Caracol' link) at Puente del Mico beside Terminal Norte. It describes continuing via Avenida Regional near TCC and Calle 67/Avenida Barranquilla before returning to Autopista Norte. The announcement gives **no verified detour end date**. This is a temporary physical route event, not a full legal 20.65-km shape or a new frequency.
2. **28 February 2026: New airport-area bus stop**. [Alcaldía de Medellín's official mobility announcement](https://www.medellin.gov.co/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/cambio-vial-y-nueva-senalizacion-garantizan-movilidad-segura-en-alrededores-del-aeropuerto-olaya-herrera-de-medellin/) states a new stop was arranged for routes 304, 305 and 313 near Olaya Herrera airport. The exact latitude/longitude, served direction and ArcGIS stop identifier are not stated. The nearby one-way change on Carrera 65G between Calles 16A/14 is a separate road measure; it cannot automatically be treated as route 313 geometry.
3. **Third-party stop order as a research aid, not a new legal route**. [Moovit's public route 313 page](https://moovitapp.com/index/es-419/transporte_p%C3%BAblico-line-313-Medellin-1642-857167-316763418-0), observed 18 November 2026, lists **32 stop positions**, a loop from Terminal Norte via Autopista Norte, Carrera 65, Olaya Herrera, Terminal Sur, Monterrey, Industriales and Carlos E. Restrepo to Terminal Norte. The exact stop names and order were saved in [`circular313_2026_third_party_stop_order_candidates.csv`](circular313_2026_third_party_stop_order_candidates.csv), with **no invented GPS/ArcGIS stop IDs**. The same service's third-party PDF fetched at this time displayed **31 stops**, so the list length is a **live-source discrepancy**, not a legal count; the two Olaya Herrera listings also need distinct stop geolocation to establish their meaning.

Moovit also reports a generic 05:00–04:55 'all-day' schedule and 5-minute weekday headway, whereas the city's official 2025 decision has weekday service windows 04:00–22:00 and day-specific maximum **peak/valley** intervals (7/15/8 workdays; 12/15/12 Sunday/holiday). The two data products **conflict**, and the third-party information is **not** grounds to override the municipal authorization or generate 24-hour trips.
