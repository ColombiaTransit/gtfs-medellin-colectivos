# Medellín Bus Companies — Official Route Documents

This table extends the company/route inventory in
[`medellin_bus_empresas_routes.md`](medellin_bus_empresas_routes.md) with
official administrative documents published by the Alcaldía de Medellín /
Secretaría de Movilidad.

The **Código(s) de ruta (repo)** column preserves the route codes from the
repository source table. The **Official route(s) in document** column records
the routes explicitly mentioned by the administrative document. These are not
assumed to be identical because route codes and variants changed over time.

This is **step 1 only**: identifying official administrative sources. Route
frequencies, schedules, dispatch intervals and detailed technical fichas will
be extracted later.

## Additional official evidence discovered

### Sistema de Rutas 6A

Gaceta Oficial 4327 contains a cluster of 2015 resolutions covering the same
Sistema de Rutas 6A:

- Resolución 1047 — Flota La V
- Resolución 1048 — Flota La Milagrosa
- Resolución 1049 — Copatra
- Resolución 1151 — Cootranscataluña
- Resolución 1046 — Coopcerquin

The official Gaceta contents identify the common route set as 097, 099, 102,
102A, 110, 111, 112, 113, 113B and 113M. citeturn3search0

### Sistema de Rutas 6C

Gaceta Oficial 4325 identifies the Sistema 6C as routes 6C1 through 6C9.
The participating companies in the approved ATCEX C6 agreement are Expreso
Campo Valdés, Autocol, Cooptransnor and Transconor. citeturn5search0

The same Gaceta identifies Resolución 1042 for Transconor and Resolución 1043
for Expreso Campo Valdés. citeturn6search0

### Sistema de Rutas 6B

Gaceta Oficial 4325 identifies Resolución 1159 as the restructuring act for
routes 002, 006, 022, 023, 024, 041A and 042. Resolución 1160 covers
Transportes Aranjuez Santa Cruz and Resolución 1161 covers Combuses. citeturn6search0

### Cootransvi / Cootransi / Coopetransa

The official Secretaría de Movilidad management index adds later administrative
history for the Sistema 6H and 6E operators. For Cootransi it identifies
routes 103 Las Mirlas, 103 La Sierra, 107 Perlas and 107 Ratón and resolutions
from 2017, 2020 and 2023. For Cootransvi it identifies the 2017 and 2019
resolutions for the same Sistema 6H. For Coopetransa it identifies the 2017
Sistema 6E agreement and the 2023 modification concerning route 069. citeturn9search7

### Cootransblan

The official 2015 Gaceta contains a substantial history for routes 246, 246i
and 246ii, including earlier resolutions fixing schedules and dispatch
frequencies. It also records later 2015 modifications to route 246. citeturn0search5turn0search4

### Cootracovi

The Alcaldía later published a specific 2022 announcement concerning route
085. It states that the official service window was expanded from 06:00–10:00
to **03:00–20:00**. This is useful later for the operational extraction
stage, but it is intentionally not mixed into the source-index table as a
frequency value. citeturn1search0

## Current source status

The table now contains substantially more official-source coverage than the
previous version. The most productive source is the 2015 Gaceta material,
particularly Gacetas **4325 and 4327**, because a single Gaceta contains
multiple company-specific resolutions.

Rows still marked **Official route document still to identify** should be
treated as unresolved rather than inferred from similar companies.
